# Getting started

New here? The one-page [quick start](quick-start.md) gets you running. The step-by-step [onboarding guide](ONBOARDING.md) has every detail. Replacing the Mac? See [New Mac](new-mac.md).

Tandem keeps your desk on your Mac. Every download includes the same trading and research tools. Only you can turn on real money per account. Practice is available where supported.

## Install and open

1. Open the Tandem disk image (`Tandem-x.y.z.dmg`) from the download page.
2. Drag Tandem to Applications.
3. Open Tandem from Applications. The release is signed with a Developer ID and notarized by Apple, so macOS opens it without extra steps. The first time, macOS asks once to confirm you want to open an app downloaded from the internet; click Open.

Tandem then lives in the menu bar. Closing the window keeps it running; use the menu bar icon to open the desk again. To uninstall, see [Remove Tandem](remove-tandem.md).

## First run

First launch guides you through an account read and a Practice preview. You can explore the sample desk before connecting anything.

1. **Connect one account.** Add an account for a venue you use. Saving keys does not activate Real. Choose **Finish later** to connect later in **Settings → Accounts & keys**.
2. **Your first account read.** Check that Tandem can show the balance for the account you connected.
3. **Preview a Practice order.** Check its quantity, limit price and maximum cost. The example sends nothing.
4. **You’re set.** Choose **Set up AI** to set up Tandem's assistant or connect an external AI app. Each path works on its own. You can also pair your iPhone, choose a starter plan or open Tandem and come back later. [Connect your AI](connect-ai.md)

From the sample banner, choose **Take the tour** for the walkthrough. It opens sample stories, dates, Copy Decisions, recurring buys and Practice records. On your own desk, press **⌘K** and type the workflow name, or use **Search help**.

Tandem saves setup progress. Return through **Settings → Accounts & keys → Reopen setup**. Choose **Finish** or **Redo** next to the step you want. The checklist shows **Not read** when a check is unavailable.

In the sample desk, open **Following → Test this source → Open Research** for a saved replay. Open **AI assistant → Desk team** for a sample discussion and its run history.

## Choose your next task

- Use the [AI assistant](trading-with-ai.md) for sourced answers, manual trade review and automatic orders within a saved grant. A tested AI connection and reviewed route are needed for inference.
- Connect an external AI app with [MCP setup](mcp-setup.md). Choose Research to read, Review to check proposals yourself, or Automatic with explicit accounts, limits and an expiry.
- Open [Markets](markets.md) for quotes, order tickets and prediction research: spreads, fair value, weather edge and wallet analysis.
- Open **Following** to save a trader or portfolio and read its Copy Decisions. Open **Strategies → Presets** for [strategy templates](presets-and-traders.md).
- Use Routines and Signals for scheduled work, permitted public watchers and the inbox. Keep Tandem running for its built-in scheduler; inspect run history to verify what actually happened.
- Use the account ticket for supported protective orders, the trade time machine and optional Telegram approval. See [Trading with your AI](trading-with-ai.md).

## Keep your records

Do not reset an account to fix a connection problem. Use [Troubleshooting](troubleshooting.md). An update or uninstall should preserve your private records; check the release's instructions before removing data.

Installation availability and acceptance depend on the release provided to you. Check its release notes for tested installation, first-launch and Keychain behavior; this guide does not certify a particular download.
