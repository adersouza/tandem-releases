# Quick start

## Open Tandem

Choose **Connect one account** or **Explore the sample desk**. If you are moving Macs, choose **Restore from backup**. Use **Finish later** to leave setup and **Settings → Accounts & keys → Reopen setup** to return.

## Connect one account

In **Settings → Accounts & keys**, choose **Add a connection**. Pick a venue and the matching account type. Enter keys only in Tandem. Use **Save and test connection** or **Save and test**. A connected account is ready to read. Its Real switch starts off. In setup, review the balance, mode and read time, then open and dismiss the **Practice order preview**. **Overview** points out **Activity** and **Stop** on your first visit. [Connect your accounts](connect-accounts.md)

## Try one task

Use **Markets** or **⌘K** to find an instrument. On an eligible Practice account, prepare a small order and open **Review order**. Check the account, side, amount and price. Confirm if you want to send it in Practice. Find the result in **Accounts** or **Activity**.

You can also try **Following → Follow a portfolio**. Check the source and its date. A saved copy starts with trading off.

## Ask a question

In **AI assistant**, select **Research** and ask “What needs my attention today?” If you haven't connected a model, open **Settings → AI** first. **Overview → Needs you** also shows the items the desk has found. [Ask your AI](ask-your-ai.md)

## Know where to stop and get help

**Stop new trades** pauses entries and attempts to cancel resting buys. Check the result in Tandem or at the venue. To trade Real, you have to enable it yourself on the exact account.

In **Settings → Support**, use **Open Help**. On the phone, use **More → Help**. Choose **Search help** or read the [Tandem client guide](client-guide.md). To save a local diagnostics file, use **Settings → Support → Diagnostics → Save diagnostics**. For support, email contact@monchera.com.
