# Quick start

## Open Tandem

Choose **Connect one account**. If you are moving Macs, choose **Restore from backup**. Use **Finish later** to leave setup and **Settings › Accounts and keys › Reopen setup** to return.

## Connect one account

In **Settings › Accounts and keys**, choose **Add a connection**. Pick a venue and the matching account type. Enter keys only in Tandem. Use **Save and test connection** or **Save and test**. A connected account is ready to read. In setup, review the balance and read time, then accept the terms on **Done** to start trading. Every manual order is reviewed before it is sent. **Overview** points out **Activity** and **Stop** on your first visit. [Connect your accounts](connect-accounts.md)

## Try one task

Use **Markets** or **⌘K** to find an instrument. Prepare a small order and open **Review order**. Check the account, side, amount and price. Confirm to send it. Find the result in **Accounts** or **Activity**.

You can also try **Following › Follow a portfolio**. Check the source and its date. A saved copy starts with trading off.

## Ask a question

In **AI assistant**, select **Research** and ask “What needs my attention today?” If you haven't connected a model, open **Settings › AI** first. **Overview › Needs you** also shows the items the desk has found. [Ask your AI](ask-your-ai.md)

## Know where to stop and get help

**Stop new trades** pauses entries and attempts to cancel resting buys. Check the result in Tandem or at the venue. A connected account trades once you accept the terms; your limits and Stop apply to every order.

In **Settings › Support**, use **Open Help**. On the phone, use **More › Help**. Choose **Search help** or read the [Tandem client guide](client-guide.md). To save a local diagnostics file, use **Settings › Help and support › Diagnostics › Save diagnostics**. For support, email contact@monchera.com.
