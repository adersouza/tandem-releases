# Fund your accounts

Tandem never moves money. It can't deposit, withdraw or transfer anything, and the keys you give it never include transfer or withdrawal access. You add money at each venue yourself, in that venue's own app or website.

## Practice needs no money

Practice accounts (Kalshi demo, Webull paper) use play money from the venue. You can run the whole practice week without funding anything.

## When you're ready for real money

1. Open the venue's own app or website and sign in. Don't use a link from an email or a message.
2. Find its deposit screen and follow its steps. Each venue checks your identity and bank in its own way.
3. Wait until the venue shows the money as available. Deposits can take time to clear.
4. Back in Tandem, open Accounts and press **Check account**. The new balance shows once the venue reports it.

Where to deposit:

- **Kalshi:** in the Kalshi app or on kalshi.com.
- **Webull:** in the Webull app. Webull crypto uses the same Webull account.
- **Polymarket US:** in the Polymarket US iPhone app. Polymarket US is real money from the start.

## Real money stays off until you turn it on

Funding an account doesn't turn real money on. Each account stays off until you open its Review step on Accounts, check its limits, and confirm. Your limits and the crypto cap apply from the first order.

To take money out, use the venue's own app. Stop new trades in Tandem first if you're closing an account.
