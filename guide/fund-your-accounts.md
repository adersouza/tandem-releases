# Fund your accounts

In Tandem, choose **Add money** beside a connected account on **Accounts**, on its account page or in **More**. The link opens the venue in your browser. Tandem moves money only on Real Coinbase accounts, with a Confirm each time ([Move money on Coinbase](coinbase-money.md)). Keep transfer and withdrawal permissions off on Tandem's keys at every other venue, and on Coinbase until you want those actions.

## Real accounts

- **Kalshi** — Open Transfers and choose Add funds. For Perps, open the Kalshi margin account and choose Deposit. [Kalshi transfers](https://help.kalshi.com/en/articles/13823791-transfers-faq) · [Kalshi Perps funding](https://help.kalshi.com/en/articles/15357664-depositing-transferring-withdrawing)
- **Polymarket US** — Open the Polymarket US iPhone app and deposit there. The browser link opens Polymarket US so you can get the app. [Polymarket US app guide](https://help.polymarket.com/en/articles/15458828-website-vs-app-what-s-the-difference)
- **Alpaca** — Sign in to the Alpaca dashboard and choose Live Trading, then Funds & Wallet. [Alpaca funding guide](https://alpaca.markets/learn/fund-live-trading-account)
- **Webull** — Sign in to My Account, choose Transfer Money and select the account to fund. [Webull deposits](https://www.webull.com/help/faq/269-ACH-Deposits)
- **Tradier** — Sign in to the Tradier dashboard, select your brokerage account and open Funding. [Tradier funding guide](https://support.tradier.com/kb/guide/en/funding-your-account-qr9YSPZrJM/Steps/4605819)

Wait until the venue shows the funds as available. In Tandem, open the account's **More › Manage this account** and choose **Check account**. Adding funds leaves your Real money switch and saved limits as you set them. **Needs you** can show **Add money** when an enabled strategy, including a followed portfolio, has a priced next order and the account's reported buying power is too low.
